## Notes

- Content comes from a field test of r1 on two real migrations: zig-fping (~4.3k lines) and
  zig-libs (249 modules, constant-time crypto, big-endian cross targets). Raw findings and the
  reports they came from were kept by the maintainer outside this repository.
- New in the skill: custom build steps (makeFn) are removed in 0.17 and the replacement pattern;
  configure-time read declarations; correction of r1's claim that builtin.cpu/os moved; array and
  vector @bitCast is little-endian on every target (the big-endian double-swap trap, verified at
  run time on s390x under qemu); byte-array <-> wide-vector @bitCast compiles to a byte loop on
  LLVM 22 and the @Vector(n,u8) spelling that does not; LLVM 22 constant-time traps (a branch on
  an `or` of two bits split into two jumps; a secret mask turned into a jump) and the asm barrier;
  the comptime string-repeat helper must be `inline fn`; Reader.allocRemaining(.limited(n)) accepts
  exactly n bytes; SafeAllocator behaviour of std.testing.allocator and per-seed allocators;
  checkAllAllocationFailures and in-place resizes; @hasDecl sees only pub declarations; Io.VTable
  net* entries moved into operate; a std rename table; a table of 0.17.0 issues fixed on the
  0.17.x branch, each labelled with its commit and "per the commit message, not reproduced here"
  unless reproduced.
- The std-net.md 0.15 section is kept but every block that does not compile on 0.17.0 is labelled
  as such; deleting it is left for a later release.
