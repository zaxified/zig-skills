## Notes

- Content comes from four real migrations to Zig 0.17.0 (a 249-module library collection and
  three applications) plus requests filed by their sessions. Raw findings, the reviews and the
  probes were kept by the maintainer outside this repository.
- New in the skill: the open compiler bug #37131 (`@intCast`/`std.math.cast` from a signed integer
  to a narrower unsigned one emits `trunc nsw`; range checks on the upper half are folded away in
  optimized builds) with the cast-shape table, the std places it reaches and range-checked
  spellings; std TLS's missing CA check on issuers and the self-signed pinning recipe with its
  limits; single-byte repeat via `@splat`; `std.ascii.indexOfIgnoreCase*` removed; a codemod recipe
  for the deprecated-call renames; replacing `init.gpa` and `init.io` together; vtable overrides by
  name; `b.addTranslateC` without a dependency; capped decompression (zstd needs its own window
  buffer); allocator behaviour (SafeAllocator buckets, `testing.allocator` reuse, smp caveats).
- std-net.md lost its 0.15.x reference sections; the three blocks that compile on 0.17.0 stay
  under "Stream I/O".
